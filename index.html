ENTRY 01 / MATHEMATICS IN NATURE

The Calm of Complexity

Finding mathematical order in something as ordinary as a tree.

PAGE 01
NATURE / 01
MY PHOTO — MATHEMATICS IN NATURE
✦

The idea: Mathematics does not always look like numbers on a page. Sometimes, it is quietly built into the way nature grows.

My Observation

One of the things I learned from this topic is that mathematics is everywhere. Before, I mostly associated mathematics with numbers, formulas, and solving problems. But I realized that it can also be seen in nature. The things around us have patterns, shapes, symmetry, and structures that can be observed through mathematics.

For my picture, I chose a tree because I noticed the way its branches are arranged. The branches spread outward from the trunk and become smaller as they grow toward the top. There is a repeated pattern in the way the branches grow, creating a balanced and organized structure. It reminds me of how mathematical patterns can naturally occur in living things.

Mathematics plays a role in the picture through the branching, repetition, symmetry, and spacing of the tree. Although the tree does not follow a perfect mathematical pattern, its structure shows how nature can have order and organization. The arrangement of its branches also allows the tree to spread through the available space and receive sunlight.

Why It Matters to Me

This is significant to me because it changed the way I look at ordinary things. I used to see a tree simply as a tree, but now I can notice the patterns and structures within it. It made me realize that mathematics is not only something we study in a classroom. It is also part of the natural world that surrounds us every day.

Perfect Imperfections

Mathematical terms can take on related but different meanings in other fields.

LANGUAGE / 02

Mathematical Terms Across Fields

Mathematical Symbol / DefinitionDefinition in Other FieldCommonalities & DifferencesImplications in Daily Life
Root
A number that, when multiplied by itself a certain number of times, gives another number.		In Botany, a “root” is part of a plant which is underground and helps to absorb water and other nutrients in the soil.Both refer to something important or fundamental. In mathematics, it refers to a number that produces another number, while in botany, it is a part of a plant that absorbs water and nutrients.The word “root” reminds us that things can have a foundation or starting point. In daily life, understanding context helps us recognize what a word means.
Power
A mathematical expression that shows repeated multiplication of the same number.		In science or engineering, power is the rate of doing work or delivering energy. In political and social science, it can refer to the ability to influence and lead.Both meanings involve strength or effect. In mathematics, power describes repeated multiplication, while in other fields it describes the capacity to produce work, energy, influence, or change.Power can mean different things depending on the situation. It reminds us that ability and influence can be used in different ways and should be understood within context.
Square
A number multiplied by itself, or a geometric figure with four equal sides and four right angles.		In Architecture/Urban Planning, a square is an open public space surrounded by buildings, such as a town square.Both meanings are connected to shape and space. In mathematics, a square is a number or geometric figure; in urban planning, it refers to a physical public space.The same word can describe an abstract mathematical idea and a real place. It shows why context is important when communicating with others.
Set
A collection of distinct objects or elements considered as a group.		In Film/Television, a set is the physical location or constructed environment where a scene is filmed.Both involve a group or organized collection, but mathematics uses “set” for elements grouped together, while film uses it for a place or environment created for production.Organization and context help us understand what belongs together. The word “set” can refer to different things depending on the situation.
Union
The set containing all elements from two or more sets, without repeating elements.		In Anatomy, union means a joining or connection between two structures, such as the union of bones. In Chemistry, it can mean combining or joining substances/elements. In Law/Politics, it can refer to a formal joining or association of states, groups, or people.All meanings share the idea of joining or combining. In mathematics, union combines elements from sets without repetition; in other fields, it describes a physical, chemical, social, or formal connection.Union shows the value of bringing different things together. In daily life, cooperation and connection can create something more complete while still respecting the individual parts.

My Process

At first, the problem was really confusing. I started by identifying each nationality first, only to realize that I was getting nowhere. On my second trial, I tried focusing on the houses, but I realized that I did not even know which house should come first. On my third attempt, I labeled the empty spaces above and started by identifying the first house and the center house. From there, I focused on the house colors and used elimination to narrow down the possibilities. Then, I worked on the drinks, followed by the cigar brands and nationalities. Once I figured out the last two categories, I was finally able to crack the problem more easily.

My Challenge

One of the biggest challenges I encountered was frustration. There were moments when I kept reading the clues over and over but still could not see the answer. I also experienced some boredom because I felt like I was making little to no progress. However, instead of giving up, I started finding ways to make the problem easier for myself. I wrote everything down on paper instead of constantly switching tabs, used elimination, and applied logical reasoning. Through multiple trials and checking different possibilities, I was gradually able to move forward. At first, the process was very slow, but once I was able to build a system for solving it, everything became much faster.

My Realization

I finished the activity in 1 hour and 33 minutes. Honestly, I think that was quite slow, but I also consider the time I spent feeling drained, zoning out, and getting frustrated because I could not immediately find the answer. Despite that, I pushed myself to keep looking for different ways to solve the problem instead of immediately relying on AI. In the end, I finally got the German as the owner of the fish, and I was genuinely happy about it. I am glad that I stopped myself from using AI when I was tempted to, because solving it on my own made the result feel much more rewarding.

My biggest realization from this activity is that there is often a solution to every problem; you just have to be patient and diligent enough to find it. Problem-solving is not always about getting the answer quickly. Sometimes, it is about being willing to try different approaches, make mistakes, eliminate possibilities, and keep going even when the answer is not immediately clear. I also realized that doing something through my own effort can be more fulfilling than simply getting the answer from AI. Overall, this was a challenging but refreshing activity that reminded me of the value of patience, logical reasoning, and perseverance.
